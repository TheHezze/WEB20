G2 and G Mock Tests on Real Routes

Experience realistic G2 or G road-test conditions with ACE’s new Mock Road Test service. Practise on real Windsor DriveTest-area routes with a certified instructor who has 20 years of experience. After your mock test, you’ll receive a detailed score sheet similar to the one used during an official road test, showing what you did well and which mistakes you need to improve. Sessions normally run rain or shine, subject to safe road and weather conditions. Choose a 20-minute session meeting at the Windsor DriveTest Centre for $40, or a 45-minute session with pickup and drop-off within Windsor for $80. Available Saturdays by appointment.

Please note: This service is a mock examination, not a driving lesson. During the mock test, your instructor will observe and assess your driving without coaching, correcting, or providing pointers while you drive. Afterward, you will receive feedback and a detailed score sheet showing your strengths and the areas you need to improve.

The assessment is designed around the skills currently evaluated during Ontario road tests. The G2 mock test may cover turns, stops, intersections, lane changes, parallel parking, three-point turns, one-way streets, residential driving, traffic lights, and stop and yield signs. The G mock test may cover advanced driving on major roads and expressways, including merging, entering and exiting the expressway, maintaining appropriate speed and following distance, signalling, turns, curves, lane changes, intersections, and business-area driving. The route and individual tasks may vary because of traffic, weather, safety conditions, and current DriveTest requirements.

Driving With a Disability: Private Lessons

ACE provides one-on-one adaptive driving lessons in Windsor for drivers with disabilities, people returning to driving after an injury or trauma, and learners experiencing driving anxiety. Training may include hand controls, foot-operated adaptations, one-handed driving techniques, and other modified-vehicle equipment. Instruction is provided by a licensed adaptive-driving instructor and tailored to each learner’s abilities, experience, equipment requirements, and driving goals.

Where appropriate, ACE works with Interactive Rehab & Driving Anxiety Solution to support clients seeking driving-anxiety and desensitization programs, behind-the-wheel retraining after injury or trauma, vehicle-modification assessments, and MTO functional driving assessments. Please contact our office to discuss your individual needs, equipment availability, and pricing.
